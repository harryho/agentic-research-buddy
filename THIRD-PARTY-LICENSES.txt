platforms plus a DuckDB JDBC driver that this code never uses. The installer profile drops
the Linux/macOS natives and the DuckDB driver from the bundle (about 112 MB); the Windows
OpenBLAS/ARPACK builds stay, because Smile's BLAS bindings load them at runtime.
